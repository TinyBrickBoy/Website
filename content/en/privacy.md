## In short

This website uses **no cookies**, **no tracking**, **no analytics**, **no external fonts** and **no CDNs**. All files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, font) are served from the same server. I do not create user profiles.

## Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by: {{< hoster >}}

When you visit the website, the server processes technically necessary data that your browser sends automatically: IP address, date and time of the request, requested page, amount of data transferred, browser and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the website and keep the server secure. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and reliable operation). The log files are not combined with other data and are deleted after a short time.

## Local storage in your browser

The website stores a single entry on your device only:

- `konsole-boot` (sessionStorage): remembers that the boot sequence already ran; deleted automatically when you close the tab

This entry is never sent to any server. It is strictly necessary for the function you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

## Stats (GitHub, Codeberg, RIPEstat)

The numbers shown by the `stats` and `asn` commands (repos, contributions per day, routing data for AS210881) are fetched from GitHub, Codeberg and RIPEstat when the website is built and written into the page. Your browser does **not** connect to these services.

## External links

Links to other websites (e.g. GitHub, Codeberg) are only opened when you click them. From then on, that provider's privacy policy applies. No referrer is sent.

## Contact by e-mail

If you e-mail me, I only process your data to answer your request (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention periods apply.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Just send me an e-mail.
