A small desktop companion for Linux Wayland desktops. The slime rests at the top centre of the screen, dodges aside when you hover over it, can be dragged around and opens a panel of handy tools on click.

## Details

- Moves along the top, left and right screen edges
- Glides to the nearest edge after dragging, across multiple monitors
