Get a free subdomain for your website, project or homelab. Everything runs through GitHub: open a pull request with one small JSON file, a validation bot checks it, a maintainer merges and the DNS records go live.

## Highlights

- No account, no payment, no dashboard
- Domains: **addictedto.beer** and **skillissue.gg**
- Ownership is tied to your GitHub username, so only you can change your entry
- Nested subdomains with additional files
